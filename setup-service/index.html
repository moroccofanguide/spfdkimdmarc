How it works

  1. Send your domain. Use one of the buttons above, or write to help@spfdkimdmarc.com with your domain and the services that send your email.
  2. Free audit. We check your current records and reply with what is wrong and the plan that fits. You only pay if you go ahead.
  3. Records delivered. You get the exact DNS entries and where to paste them in your DNS panel, or we publish them with temporary access.
  4. Verification. We confirm that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass on real messages, and fix anything that does not.

Questions

Do you need access to my DNS?

No. By default we send you the exact records and where to paste them, and check the result once you have published them. If you prefer, you can give temporary access to your DNS panel and we publish them for you.

Which providers do you support?

Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho Mail, OVHcloud and the usual extra senders: newsletter tools, CRMs, helpdesks, invoicing software and websites sending mail from their own server.

How do I pay?

After the free audit you receive a quote with the scope and payment details. Nothing is due before you agree.

What if my email still goes to spam afterwards?

Authentication is the base, but content and sending reputation also matter. If the records pass and delivery is still poor, we tell you what else we see and what it would take to fix it.
