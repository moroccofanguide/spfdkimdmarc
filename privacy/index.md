# Privacy policy

> What spfdkimdmarc.com collects: no accounts, no cookies, cookieless visit statistics, DNS lookups from your browser or our MCP endpoint.

URL: https://spfdkimdmarc.com/privacy/  
Updated: 2026-10-23

Last updated 2026-10-23

## What this site collects

Nothing that identifies you while you browse. There are no accounts, no forms sent to our servers, no cookies and no advertising trackers. Visits are counted with a cookieless analytics tool (see below).

## If you email us

When you write to help@spfdkimdmarc.com, for example to request the setup service, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it only to answer you and to carry out the work you ask for, and we do not share it.

## DNS lookups

When you check a domain, your browser sends DNS queries for that domain directly to Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (`cloudflare-dns.com`), or to Google Public DNS (`dns.google`) if Cloudflare does not answer. These requests go from your browser to those services, not through us. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

## AI assistants (MCP)

Since 2026-10-23, AI assistants can run the same checks through our MCP endpoint (`https://spfdkimdmarc.com/mcp`) or, on the home page, through the browser's WebMCP interface. On the MCP endpoint, the assistant sends us the domain (and DKIM selector) to check; our server, a Cloudflare Worker, queries the same public DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers and returns the result. We do not store these requests or their results, and we do not log them. Cloudflare processes them as our host (see Hosting). WebMCP checks run in your browser, like the regular checker.

## Visit statistics

Since 2026-10-22 the site uses [Ahrefs Web Analytics](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10247870-about-ahrefs-web-analytics) to count visits. According to Ahrefs, it sets no cookie, does not store IP addresses and does not track a visitor from one day to the next. It records the pages viewed, the referring site, your country, your browser and your device type.

## Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, behind Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers may process technical data such as your IP address in their server logs to deliver and protect the site.

## Changes

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added, this page will be updated first and the date above changed.
