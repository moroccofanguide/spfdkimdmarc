What this site collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no forms sent to our servers, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

DNS lookups

When you check a domain, your browser sends DNS queries for that domain directly to Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver (cloudflare-dns.com), or to Google Public DNS (dns.google) if Cloudflare does not answer. These requests go from your browser to those services, not through us. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, behind Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers may process technical data such as your IP address in their server logs to deliver and protect the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if analytics or advertising are added, this page will be updated first and the date above changed.
